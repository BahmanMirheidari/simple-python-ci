Dementia is a general term for a decline in
cognitive function that affects daily activities.

Common cognitive assessment methods include:
the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE),
the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA),
and the Addenbrooke's Cognitive Examination (ACE).

Cognitive assessments may evaluate memory,
attention, language, executive function
and visuospatial abilities.

Speech-based assessment can also provide
information about cognitive and linguistic changes.
